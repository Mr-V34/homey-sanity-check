Know why your Homey Pro restarted. Sanity Check tells a planned restart from an unexpected one, and after an unexpected restart it looks back at the minutes before it: which apps crashed, which ones behaved unusually, and whether something scheduled ran at the same time. You also see which apps and firmware have updates waiting.

The results land on your timeline, with a daily health check each morning and Flow cards to act on restarts, crashes and reports. Everything is an indication, not proof: Sanity Check reports what happened at the same time and never claims a cause. It only reads from Homey and never changes anything.
