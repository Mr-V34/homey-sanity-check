Know why your Homey Pro restarted. Sanity Check tells a planned restart from an unexpected one, and after an unexpected restart it looks back at the minutes before it: which apps crashed, which ones behaved unusually, and whether something scheduled ran at the same time. You also see which apps and firmware have updates waiting, and get a warning when backups have stopped working.

The results land on your timeline, with a daily health check each morning and Flow cards to act on restarts, crashes and reports. After an unexpected restart you get a short checklist (app crashes, apps, power, temperature, load) with a likely cause and advice, for example to contact Athom support when nothing points at an app, power or heat. It is advice, not proof: every line says what was measured. Homey says the app can manage your entire Homey because seeing other apps' crashes requires that permission; Sanity Check only reads, never changes anything and sends nothing outside your Homey.
